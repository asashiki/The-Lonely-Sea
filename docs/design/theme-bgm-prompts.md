# The Lonely Sea：四主题循环 BGM

四首保持同一套稀疏、平静的音色，只改变光线与温度。每条都可单独复制。以下是生成提示词，不是已经生成或试听过的音频。

## 雾昼 / Morning Mist

Create a seamless-looping minimalist ambient BGM for the Japanese visual-novel-style title menu of a personal blog called “The Lonely Sea.” A pale blue-gray coast rests in morning mist; old photographs and handwritten memories lie on the shore. Quiet, spacious, slightly cool, gently lonely, with a small trace of warmth. Use only sparse felt piano, a nearly inaudible airy pad, and distant soft ocean waves. A simple three-note piano motif with long breathing spaces, around 64 BPM. Use suspended harmony, gentle seconds and sixths, without a strong cadence. Keep the same restrained energy throughout. No drums, vocals, orchestral strings, busy melody, dramatic development, build-up, climax, or cinematic swell. About 2 minutes 30 seconds. Begin already inside the musical loop, without an introduction. Let the ending reconnect naturally to the opening in harmony, wave ambience, and reverb. This is unobtrusive menu music, not a song with a beginning, middle, and ending.

## 晴昼 / Quiet Daylight

Create a seamless-looping minimalist ambient BGM for the daylight theme of “The Lonely Sea,” a personal blog presented as a quiet Japanese visual-novel title menu. Clear pale-blue water, mild sunlight, an open window, and a notebook on a wooden table. Peaceful and lightly warm, never cheerful or energetic. Use soft felt piano, a very faint warm sustained pad, and distant gentle surf. A small three- or four-note motif, around 68 BPM, with generous silence between phrases. Favor open major-add9 and sixth harmonies, played softly without resolution or a sense of forward drive. Keep the texture thin and the loudness nearly constant. No drums, claps, plucked rhythmic patterns, sparkling arpeggios, orchestral arrangement, vocals, crescendos, or climax. About 2 minutes 30 seconds. Start in a stable musical state and return invisibly to it at the loop point. Make it comfortable beneath reading for an hour: a quiet daylight room beside the sea, not an uplifting soundtrack.

## 静夜 / Still Night

Create a seamless-looping minimalist ambient BGM for the night theme of “The Lonely Sea,” a personal blog with the atmosphere of a Japanese visual-novel title menu. Deep blue water beneath moonlight, a distant lighthouse, and a small pool of lamplight over a diary. Intimate, calm, cool but safe; solitude without sadness. Use sparse muted felt piano in the middle register, a very soft dark-blue ambient pad, and distant low ocean waves. A slow three-note motif, around 60 BPM, with long pauses. Use open fifths, gentle suspended chords, and occasional soft ninths. Avoid strong minor-key melancholy, unresolved suspense, or very deep rumbling bass. Maintain the same small scale and quiet intensity throughout. No percussion, vocals, strings, elaborate melody, dramatic changes, build-up, or climax. About 2 minutes 30 seconds, with no introduction or ending. Match the final harmony and reverb to the opening for an unobtrusive continuous loop. It should feel like quietly staying awake beside the sea.

## 赤夜 / Ember Night

Create a seamless-looping minimalist ambient BGM for the crimson-night theme of “The Lonely Sea,” a personal blog styled as a Japanese visual-novel title menu. A still dark sea reflects a muted red moon; inside the lighthouse, the last warmth of a lamp rests on an old letter. Quiet, private, warm-dark, slightly unusual, but entirely peaceful. Crimson is a color here, not danger, horror, passion, or an approaching dramatic event. Use sparse warm felt piano, an extremely soft low-mid ambient pad, and distant subdued surf. A simple three-note motif around 62 BPM. Let a gentle minor-sixth or add9 color occasionally shade otherwise open, suspended harmony. No ominous drones, dissonant stingers, pulsing bass, percussion, vocals, orchestral strings, crescendos, dramatic development, or climax. Keep the same restrained volume and texture for about 2 minutes 30 seconds. Begin within the loop and reconnect the ending naturally to the opening. A calm room under red moonlight, not a cinematic scene.
