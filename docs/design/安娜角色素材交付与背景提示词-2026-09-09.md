## 可以发给角色生图 AI 的要求

使用我提供的**最新修改版安娜参考图**，保持角色脸型、发型、服装、帽子、配色和画风一致。不要自行恢复旧版角色设定。本批用于文字视觉小说的事件镜头与人物构图，不是带字海报，不要画台词、气泡、文字、UI、框线。

共列六个用途，已有图能准确覆盖的不用重画。先确认第一张，随后以它为参考生成其他图。不要六张各自换一组画风。

### 尺寸与构图

- 普通人物使用竖图，建议约 1024×1536 或相近比例，保留原始清晰文件。站椅图也用竖图，完整包含脚和椅子。不要为了指定分辨率过度放大或重复抽图。
- 人物在画布中间即可，周围留少量边距；**不要再把人物缩到横图右边、左边留一大片透明空间**。人物站哪一边由游戏合成控制。
- 不同姿势不要求脚底机械对齐，但同一机位下脸部的大小要接近；透视差异交给明确的新镜头，不靠偶然生成。
- 统一柔和光线，不烘焙强烈蓝光、树影、耀斑或文字遮罩。透明背景最好；如果暂时做不到，底色由你按抠图流程决定，不要求绿色或其他指定抠像底色。
- 每张独立 PNG，命名后放一个文件夹。不要每张一个文件夹。
- 特写先从原图试裁切。只有缺清晰度或需要不同角度时，再补一张专用面部近景，不现在批量生成眼睛、手、嘴等局部。
- 不要求嘴型差分。当前任务是 NVL 演出，不自动沿用 ADV 的持续口型系统。

先发01和05的样张确认脸部连续性与光线，认可后再补其他用途。新版角色图未交付前，不把旧版视为最终外观。

### 房间图实际提示词

Edit the provided image, preserving the exact wide framing and illustrated Japanese visual-novel background style. Change ONLY the luminous display surface inside the central monitor: replace its bright white notes/chat interface with a low-brightness charcoal/slate dark-theme code editor, with a few subdued tiny lines of code-like marks, barely legible and not intended to be read. Keep the monitor clearly on but softly lit, no white panels or neon syntax. Slightly reduce ONLY the immediate monitor light spill as appropriate. Preserve the monitor bezel, geometry, every room object, posters, figurines, cups, keyboard, microphone, window, moon and existing warm lamp/cool night lighting unchanged. No new objects, no people, no global recoloring or darkening. Deliver one full-size landscape background image, not a before/after comparison.

### 事务所图实际提示词

Generate ONE new 16:9 landscape illustration, a quiet small detective agency interior at night, for an NVL visual novel. The reference image is STYLE AND LIGHTING REFERENCE ONLY, not the room to copy. Match its understated Japanese galgame background painting: controlled fine contours, soft matte painted surfaces, restrained detail, muted blue-gray nighttime atmosphere with a small gentle warm lamp accent, ordinary believable furniture, not photoreal or glossy 3D, no extravagant cinematic lighting. New scene: modest desk along the left side with a few loose manuscript sheets, a magnifying glass and a teacup; two small dim dark monitors farther back, a simple bookshelf and an unobtrusive window with dark blue night beyond. Eye-level view into the room, middle and right foreground open for later character compositing, furniture not blocking the whole foreground, useful low-detail central wall area for overlaid NVL text. Warmth but not lavish or magical. Quiet balanced contrast: no bright white screens, no brilliant moon, no bloom, particles or sunbeams. No people, no anime posters or figurines copied from the reference, no readable writing, captions, logos, dialogue boxes or borders. The environment should support the characters and reading, not compete with them. Deliver one complete opaque background, not a collage or multiple views.
